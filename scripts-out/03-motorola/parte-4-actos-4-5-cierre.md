## ACT V — WHAT WAS LEFT

>> Photo: Motorola RAZR V3, closed, black, on a plain surface

In 2004 Motorola shipped a phone thirteen point nine millimetres thick, cut from aluminium, with an etched keypad that glowed blue. The RAZR V3.

>> Photo: Motorola RAZR V3, open

It is probably the most beautiful object of its decade. Motorola sold more than a hundred million.

Behind it there was nothing. It ran software nobody outside Motorola wrote for. When it got old the only move was price.

There was no second act, because the second act would have been a platform, and Motorola built objects.

>> GEN:curva-cuota-2006-2009 — la caída del 22 % al 6 %, con las fechas

Its own filings: about twenty-two per cent of the world handset market in 2006, about six in the last quarter of 2008.

>> Silence. GEN:parrafo-10k-2009 — el párrafo del informe anual, sin la cifra

In the 2009 report the number is gone. The sentence reads: our share of the worldwide wireless handset market has declined significantly in the last several years.

>> Photo: Motorola Solutions wordmark

In January 2011 the company split in two. Solutions kept the radios.

>> GEN:google-lenovo-flujo — 12.500 M → 2.910 M, y las patentes desviándose

Google bought Mobility for twelve and a half billion dollars, sold it to Lenovo for two point nine one, and kept most of the patents.

>> Black

The patents were the point. Nobody wanted the phones.

## Huecos GEN

Encargo para Nano Banana. **Nada integrado.** Regla dura del §6 del plan: ningún
`GEN:` reconstruye **personas reales en hechos reales**. Aquí no hay un solo rostro
identificable, ni una rueda de prensa, ni un consejo de administración. Solo
objetos, lugares vacíos, esquemas y tipografía.

**Paleta común de la parte 4**, para que los ocho planos se lean como una familia:
negro carbón (#0E0E10) de fondo, blanco hueso (#EDE9E3) para texto y línea, un solo
acento ámbar frío (#C8842A) reservado a *lo que Motorola pierde*, y un cian apagado
(#5B8C9B) para *lo que sobrevive*. Sin degradados de color, sin brillos, sin lens
flare. Grano fino uniforme para casar con el archivo de 16 mm.

### 2. Corrección de cifras: la cuota **no** es 21 % → 6 %
